What each button does

  • Create Event — two-step form: scrim or tournament, name, date/time IST, slots, maps (Erangel / Miramar / Rondo), team size; then description/rules, region, and an optional IDP/WhatsApp group link. Opening registration prompts for a channel to post the public Register announcement.

  • View Registrations — approve, disqualify or remove team registrations per event.
  • Manage Teams / Manage Players — look up any team or player, edit, suspend, disband.
  • Announce — post an announcement embed to a chosen channel.
  • Lock / Unlock — lock or unlock a channel during live matches.
  • DM Owners — broadcast a DM to all verified team owners, with a success/failure report.
  • Logs — audit log of admin actions. Settings — General (team size, substitutes, maps, team-ID prefix, admin roles) and Registration (teams per group for auto group/slot assignment).
